These are independent samples of how I structure a public-source research brief: a bottom line, a dated chronology, actor positions, trigger indicators to monitor, conditional scenarios, and a source appendix in which every claim carries a confidence label — confirmed primary source, reported secondary source, or inferred analyst judgment. They are not commissioned work and make no forecasts.

How these are produced

I run an AI-assisted research workflow: several models work the same task in parallel in fixed roles — source collection, verification, counter-argument, citation and logic review — and challenge each other's findings. I supervise the process, resolve disagreements, and sign off. Sources are collected first, then each claim is checked against its cited document before it is written up.
