How this was produced

Sources were collected first by parallel research passes (one per section), then every claim was checked against its cited document by a separate verification pass that could downgrade or drop it. Items that failed verification were removed. Scenarios and “what a change would signal” lines are analyst judgment and are labeled inferred. Bottom line

Since October 2022 the United States has built a layered control regime on advanced AI chips and the tools that make them. The first Bureau of Industry and Security (BIS) interim final rule created new control entries (ECCNs 3A090 and 4A090) for advanced chips and added semiconductor manufacturing equipment (SME) to the Commerce Control List, with new license requirements for Chinese supercomputer and advanced-fab end uses S1 confirmed. BIS tightened the regime in October 2023, December 2024 and January 2025 S6 S9 S12 confirmed. Since 2025 the executive branch has partly reversed course: it rescinded enforcement of the AI Diffusion Rule S15 confirmed, licensed H20 sales S16 S18 confirmed, and in January 2026 moved H200/MI325X-class chips to case-by-case review S25 confirmed. One day later a Section 232 proclamation put a 25% tariff on a narrow class of advanced chips not intended for U.S. use S26 confirmed; this tariff is the reported vehicle for the U.S. government's 25% share of H200 sale proceeds S303 reported. Actual flows remain small: Nvidia reported no data-center Hopper shipments to China in the quarter ended 2026-04-26 S206 confirmed, and BIS Under Secretary Jeffrey Kessler called H200 shipments "trivial" in July 2026 S31 reported. China has responded with measures including mineral export controls S5 S10 S23 confirmed, counter-listings of U.S. entities S30 confirmed, an antitrust case against Nvidia S11 confirmed, and reported guidance steering firms away from Nvidia parts S19 reported. A Busan truce in November 2025 suspended the sharpest measures on both sides S24 confirmed; the U.S. Affiliates Rule suspension and China's two suspensions all lapse between 2026-11-09 and 2026-11-27 S203 S210 (confirmed/reported), while MOFCOM's April 2025 rare-earth controls remain in force. Inferred: the next twelve months turn on the September Xi visit, the November truce deadlines, and whether Congress codifies limits in the FY2027 National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA).
